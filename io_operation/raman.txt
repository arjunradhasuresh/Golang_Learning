The Ramayanam is an ancient Indian epic written by sage Valmiki. It narrates the life of Lord Rama, the prince of Ayodhya, who embodies virtue, duty, and honor. The story follows Rama’s exile to the forest, the abduction of his wife Sita by the demon king Ravana, and the epic battle to rescue her with the help of Hanuman and the Vanara army. The Ramayanam explores themes of dharma (righteousness), loyalty, and devotion. It is not only a tale of heroism but also a spiritual guide, revered across India and Southeast Asia for its moral and cultural values.
